# Story Template

Optional lightweight backlog tracking template. Use this when you want to
track a work item in the backlog without a full implementation plan, or as
a summary card alongside a plan.

For most non-trivial work, the plan (`.opencode/plans/`) is the primary
artifact. This story template is useful for:
- lightweight backlog items that don't need a full plan,
- tracking status of work items across sessions,
- providing a short summary card when a plan exists.

Copy this file, rename it to reflect the story (e.g. `E01S01-add-rate-limiting.md`),
and fill in the relevant sections.

---

## Story ID

`E__S__` — Epic number and story number within the epic. Assign sequentially.

## Title

One-line description of what gets built or fixed.

## Objective

One or two sentences. What problem does this solve, and for whom?

## Lot boundary

Explicit list of what is in scope and what is explicitly out of scope.

**In scope:**
- ...

**Out of scope:**
- ...

## Acceptance criteria

Each criterion must be independently verifiable. Write them as checkboxes.

- [ ] ...
- [ ] ...
- [ ] ...

## Status

`draft` | `refined` | `active` | `done` | `blocked`

## Notes

Any additional context, links to related plans, or prior decisions.
